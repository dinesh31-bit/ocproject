Our Story

Made For The Wild

Wild & Alive exists to put a camera between people and the wild places they'd otherwise never see — and to turn that attention into action for the species and habitats we film.

Our Mission

Storytelling As Conservation

We believe appreciation is the first step toward protection. Nobody fights for a place they've never felt close to, so every film, photo essay and field dispatch we publish is built to close that distance — cinematic enough to move you, honest enough to trust.

That's why Wild & Alive isn't just a magazine. A share of every partnership and subscription funds the conservation projects we cover, from coral restoration to anti-poaching patrols — so the stories we tell also help write the next chapter.

Our Journey

Milestones In The Making

  • 2016

    Founding of Wild & Alive

    Three field biologists and a camera bag became a two-person studio publishing its first short film from a rented cabin on the edge of the Amazon.

  • 2018

    First Feature Documentary

    Breath of the Canopy premiered to festival audiences, funding our first standalone conservation grant for reforestation in the Congo Basin.

  • 2020

    Global Conservation Partnership Network

    We formalized long-term partnerships with field organizations across five continents, tying every published story to on-the-ground work.

  • 2022

    Recognized For Storytelling Craft

    Our ocean series earned an international wildlife filmmaking honor, bringing new readers to the reef restoration work it documented.

  • 2025

    300 Stories & Counting

    We launched a rotating Ocean Correspondent program and crossed 300 published stories — with more field teams active than ever before.
